Manali is where most Himalayan holidays begin — and for good reason. Sitting at 2,050 m beside the Beas river in Kullu district, it pairs snow-dusted peaks with warm cafes, adventure sports and deodar forests. Families love the easy rhythm of Mall Road, Hadimba Temple and Club House; couples drift toward Old Manali's riverside walks; thrill-seekers head straight to Solang Valley's paragliding slopes and the Atal Tunnel route into Lahaul.

From Manali you can day-trip to Kasol and Manikaran's hot springs, drive up to Rohtang or Sissu, or use the town as the gateway to Spiti and Leh. Because our office, team and entire vehicle fleet are based right here in Manali, every Davis Trip holiday in the valley is run by people who know every road, hotel and shortcut by name.
