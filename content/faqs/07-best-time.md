question: What is the best time to visit Manali and the Himalayas?
order: 7
---
October to June is the broad window. December–February brings the deepest snow (perfect for snow-seekers, book 3–4 weeks early), March–June is the classic family season, and September–October offers the clearest mountain views. July–August is monsoon — we plan around it or suggest alternatives. For Spiti, the road season is June–September only.
