Shimla, the former summer capital of British India, is still the country's most loved hill station. The Mall Road and the Ridge sit above the traffic, guarded by Christ Church and centuries of colonial architecture, while the UNESCO-listed Kalka–Shimla toy train still winds up through 102 tunnels the way it has since 1903.

Around the town, Kufri's snow slopes and amusement park keep children busy, Naldehra's golf course rolls through deodar glades, and the apple orchards of Kotkhai line the drives. Most travellers pair Shimla with Manali in a single 5–6 day circuit — we run that route weekly with our own vehicles and drivers.
