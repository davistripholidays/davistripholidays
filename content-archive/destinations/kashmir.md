Kashmir delivers the postcard India is famous for: shikara rides on Dal Lake, Mughal gardens in full bloom, houseboats built of carved cedar, and meadows that turn gold by autumn. In Gulmarg, the gondola lifts you to 3,980 m on Apharwat — one of the highest cable cars on earth — while Pahalgam's Lidder valley offers pony trails, pine forests and film-set scenery.

A well-planned Kashmir trip needs 5–7 unhurried days: Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and ideally Sonmarg. We handle the itinerary, the right hotels for your group and every transfer, so you experience the valley's beauty without its logistics headaches.
