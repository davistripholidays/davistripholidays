Goa is more than its beaches — though the beaches are excellent. North Goa's Baga, Calangute, Anjuna and Vagator bring the energy: shacks, water sports, flea markets and cliff-top sunsets. South Goa answers with quiet stretches like Palolem and Agonda where you can walk for minutes without seeing another soul.

In between lie Portuguese-heritage quarters like Fontainhas in Panaji, whitewashed churches, spice plantations and the Mandovi's sunset cruises. Three to four days is the sweet spot for a recharge; five lets you add Dudhsagar falls or a slow day in the Latin quarter. We plan both quick getaways and week-long versions.
