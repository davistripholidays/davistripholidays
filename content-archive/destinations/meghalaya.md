Meghalaya might be India's most surprising state. Cherrapunji's waterfalls — led by the 340 m plunge of Nohkalikai — fall through gorges wrapped in cloud; the double-decker living root bridges at Nongriat were grown, not built, over centuries; and the Umngot river at Dawki is so clear that boats appear to float on air.

Shillong makes an easy base with its cafés and music scene, while Mawlynnong — Asia's cleanest village — shows the region's quiet pride. The Northeast rewards travellers who plan properly: permits are simple but distances and weather need local judgment, which our team provides.
