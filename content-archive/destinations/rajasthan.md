Rajasthan is India at its most cinematic — hilltop forts in Jaipur and Jodhpur, Jaisalmer's golden citadel rising out of the Thar desert, and lake palaces in Udaipur. Amber Fort's ramparts, Hawa Mahal's honeycomb facade and Mehrangarh's cliffs could fill a week on their own.

The desert completes the story: camel rides on the Sam dunes, folk musicians around a bonfire, and dinners on rooftops overlooking a living medieval city. Travel between October and March, when the weather is perfect, and let us pace the circuit so you experience royalty without exhaustion.
