Uttarakhand carries two very different holidays in one state. The Char Dham Yatra — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath — is the journey of a lifetime for pilgrims, climbing from Haridwar's ghats to shrines at 3,000 m and above. We plan the yatra with realistic pacing, comfortable night halts and drivers who know the mountain roads.

Outside yatra season, the same hills offer Mussoorie's ridge walks, Rishikesh's Ganga aarti and rafting, Nainital's lake, and Auli's ski slopes in winter. Tell us which Uttarakhand you want — temple trail or hill holiday — and we'll build it.
