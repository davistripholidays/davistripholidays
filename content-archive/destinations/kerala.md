Kerala moves at a different speed. Munnar's tea gardens roll over the Western Ghats in every shade of green; Alleppey's houseboats drift through Vembanad's backwaters past paddy fields and kingfishers; and Fort Kochi layers Dutch, Portuguese and Chinese history into a walkable waterfront.

Add Thekkady's spice plantations and wildlife sanctuary, Kovalam's crescent beaches and a Kathakali performance, and you have India's most relaxing circuit. Honeymooners and families both thrive here — we simply tune the pace, the houseboat class and the hotels to who's travelling.
