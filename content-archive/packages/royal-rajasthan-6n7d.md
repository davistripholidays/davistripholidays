Three cities, three shades of royal — Jaipur's pink, Jodhpur's blue, Jaisalmer's gold. This circuit hits the icons (Amber Fort, Mehrangarh, the golden citadel) and then goes one better: a night in a Swiss desert camp under the clearest sky you'll ever see, with folk musicians and a camel ride out to the dunes at sunset.

October to March is the window — book Diwali or New Year dates early, because heritage hotels in these three cities fill up months ahead.
