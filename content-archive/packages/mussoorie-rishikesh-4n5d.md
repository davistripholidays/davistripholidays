Two of Uttarakhand's easiest, friendliest hill towns in one easy week — Mussoorie's Mall Road nostalgia and Kempty falls, then Rishikesh's ghats, the evening Ganga aarti and India's best white-water rafting. It's the ideal first mountains trip from Delhi: short drives, big views, something for every age.

Add Nainital or Haridwar if you have a spare day or two — the route flexes either way, and our own vehicles run the whole circuit.
