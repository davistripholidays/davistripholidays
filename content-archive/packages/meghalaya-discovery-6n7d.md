The Northeast is the least-visited and most-rewarding corner of India, and this week covers its greatest hits: Cherrapunji's waterfalls, the double-decker living root bridge trek at Nongriat, Dawki's impossibly clear Umngot river and Mawlynnong — Asia's cleanest village.

Distances look short on maps and aren't in real life; Meghalaya rewards slow, well-planned routing. We build in the buffer time, the right vehicles and a local-knows-which-waterfall-is-flowing plan B for every day.
