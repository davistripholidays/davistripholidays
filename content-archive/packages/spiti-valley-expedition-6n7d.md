Spiti is the trip your WhatsApp group still talks about years later — a cold desert of thousand-year-old monasteries, villages above 4,000 m and a lake so blue it looks rendered. This expedition runs Shimla-side in and Manali-side out, so the altitude climbs gently and you actually sleep well instead of white-knuckling it.

We only run Spiti in our adventure-ready fleet — Scorpios and Gypsys with drivers who have crossed Kunzum La for a decade — and the route is planned around acclimatisation, not just distance.
